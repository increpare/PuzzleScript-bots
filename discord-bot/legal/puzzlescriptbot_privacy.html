PuzzleScriptBot privacy policy

Last updated 7 October 2026

PuzzleScriptBot is a Discord bot for playing PuzzleScript games in a channel. It is run by increpare. This page says what the bot keeps about you, and why.

What the bot keeps

  • Your score. Your Discord user id, with a list of the levels you have solved. Each entry is a game's id and a level number. This is what /rank and the rank announcements are worked out from.
  • Games. For each game: the ids of the message and channel it is in, which game it is, and the moves made in it. The bot does not record who made each move. It does keep the display name of whoever made the most recent move, which is shown under the game as "Last move".
  • Levels you send. A level made with the level editor is kept with your Discord user id and the display name you had when you sent it. The name is shown on the level.
  • Levels you solve. When you solve a level that someone sent, your user id and display name are kept with that level, and the name is shown on it.
  • The workshop. The game being edited in the shared editor, and its list of saves. These hold the game's text only, with no names or ids.
  • Error logs. Ordinary log entries hold timings and game ids only. When a request to Discord fails, the entry for it can include the ids involved, which may include your user id.

What the bot does not keep

  • It does not read or store messages. It only receives the commands and button presses sent to it.
  • It stores nothing about your keyword role. /role changes the role on Discord and nothing more.
  • It never sees your email address, and takes nothing from your Discord profile beyond the user id and display name described above.
  • It does not see your IP address. The level editor's requests reach the bot through Discord's own servers.
  • Opening the level editor signs you in through Discord so that the bot knows which user is editing. The bot uses that to read your user id and keeps nothing else from it.

What other people can see

A game, its "Last move" name, a sent level with its author and solvers, and rank announcements are posted in the channel, where everyone in that channel can see them. When you press Share in the workshop, the bot starts a game of it in a channel and says there that you shared it. Whatever you type in the workshop is seen by everyone who has it open. Replies to /rank and /role are shown only to you.

Where it is kept, and for how long

  • Everything is kept on a server run by increpare. Nothing is sold, passed on, or used for advertising or analytics.
  • Scores are kept until you ask for them to be removed.
  • Sent levels, and the names of their authors and solvers, are kept until you ask for yours to be removed.
  • Games are kept until newer ones push them out. The bot holds about 1 MB of them, and when that is full it deletes finished games first, then the ones played least recently.
  • Logs are held in the operating system's journal, which discards old entries as it fills.

Other services

  • Discord. Everything you do with the bot goes through Discord, under Discord's own privacy policy.
  • GitHub. Games are fetched from GitHub by their id. Pressing Share in the workshop publishes the game's text there as a public gist, under the bot's own GitHub account. Nothing about you is sent to GitHub.

Seeing or removing your data

You can ask for a copy of what the bot holds about you, or for it to be deleted, by opening an issue at github.com/increpare/PuzzleScript-bots. You will need to give your Discord user id.

Changes

This page is updated whenever what the bot keeps changes.
