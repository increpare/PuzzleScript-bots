Sokoban puzzles by David W. Skinner

The puzzle you were playing is one of David W. Skinner's. He made the Microban and Sasquatch sets of Sokoban puzzles between 1999 and 2010, more than a thousand puzzles in all, and gave them away to be freely distributed provided they remain properly credited.

His own page, with all of his sets as he published them:
David W. Skinner - Sokoban

Why this page is here

PuzzleScriptBot posts one of his puzzles each day as the Skinner of the Day, as a small PuzzleScript game of one level. A PuzzleScript game links to its homepage over https, and his page is not served that way, so the games link here and this page links to his.

The puzzles are his, unchanged. They are Microban I to IV and Sasquatch I to XI, numbered as he numbered them: "Microban II.3" is puzzle 3 of Microban II. The game around each puzzle is the Microban demo that comes with PuzzleScript, by increpare.
